The cyclopedia uses two icon shapes: a circle (skill) and a corner-stripe "D" (focus), colored by specialty; Any is the tricolor stripe, Variable the black "?". One discrepancy surfaced: Delta Vega and Boreth carry stripe (focus-style) icons in the cyclopedia but box1.json records them as Skills - worth a data cross-check.
